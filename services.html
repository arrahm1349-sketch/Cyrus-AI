Services

AI advisory and implementation, matched to where you actually are

Whether you're exploring your first use case or scaling AI across departments, we plug in at the stage you need — assessment, build, or ongoing advisory.

How it works

Every engagement runs through four phases

From first conversation to live results, here's exactly what working with Cyrus AI looks like.

01

Phase 1 — Free Consultation

A no-obligation conversation to understand your business, goals, and challenges — and give you an honest first view on where AI could genuinely help.

02

Phase 2 — Inspection of Business

A deep look under the hood at your processes, data, systems, and team capacity, to pinpoint exactly where AI can save time, cut cost, or unlock growth.

03

Phase 3 — Building the Solution

We design and build the right AI solution for your business — improving efficiency and increasing capacity without disrupting what already works.

04

Phase 4 — Implementation

We deploy the solution into daily operations, train your team to use it confidently, and make sure it delivers real, measurable results.

What's included

The services behind each phase

Specific capabilities we draw on as we move your business through the four phases.

AI Strategy & Readiness Assessment

A structured audit of your data, tools, and workflows. You leave with a ranked list of AI opportunities, each scored on effort, cost, and expected impact.

Process Automation

We identify repetitive, manual work — data entry, reporting, customer replies, scheduling — and automate it using AI-powered tools integrated into your existing systems.

Custom AI Solutions

When off-the-shelf tools won't cut it, we design and build bespoke AI applications — internal copilots, document processing, forecasting tools — tailored to your data.

Integration & Deployment

We connect AI tools to the systems you already run — CRM, ERP, helpdesk, spreadsheets — so adoption doesn't mean ripping out what already works.

Team Training & Enablement

Hands-on workshops and documentation so your team can use, adapt, and maintain the tools we put in place — without staying dependent on us.

Fractional AI Advisory

Ongoing, part-time access to senior AI expertise — for businesses that want a steady hand guiding decisions without hiring a full-time AI lead.
