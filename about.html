About Cyrus AI

AI advice that respects your time, budget, and existing systems

We started Cyrus AI because too much "AI advice" is either generic hype or unusable theory. We focus on what a real business can act on this quarter.

Our story

Built for businesses that need results, not buzzwords

Cyrus AI was founded to close the gap between what AI can do and what most SMEs and mid-sized organisations are actually able to use today. We've seen too many businesses buy expensive tools that never get adopted, or sit on the sidelines because "AI" feels too abstract to act on.

Our approach is deliberately narrow: find the two or three places AI will genuinely save time or money in your business, prove it works, and build your team's confidence to take it further — rather than a sprawling transformation programme that never ships.

How we work

Principles we don't compromise on

Vendor-neutral

We're not paid by any software vendor. Our recommendations are based on what fits your business, not what we sell.

ROI-first

Every recommendation comes with an honest estimate of cost, effort, and payback — including telling you when AI isn't the answer.

Hands-on delivery

We don't just advise — we build and deploy alongside your team, then train them to own it.

No lock-in

We build on tools and platforms you can maintain without us. Our goal is your team's independence, not a retainer forever.

Right-sized for you

Whether you're a 10-person business or a 500-person enterprise, we scope engagements to match your budget and risk appetite.

Responsible & transparent

We flag data, privacy, and governance risks upfront — no cutting corners to hit a deadline.
